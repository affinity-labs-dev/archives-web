Questions This Data Answers

1. How often do users visit Profile?

  1. Total profile views (30d): profile_viewed total count
  2. Daily trend: PostHog Trends → profile_viewed → Daily

2. What % expand the stats section?

  1. Stats expansion rate: profile_stats_expanded / profile_viewed
  2. Low rate may indicate stats are not discoverable or not interesting enough

3. Which achievements are most tapped?

  1. Breakdown by achievement_name: profile_achievement_tapped broken down by achievement_name
  2. Shows which achievements users are most curious about or proud of

4. Which monthly badges get the most interaction?

  1. Breakdown by month: profile_badge_tapped broken down by month
  2. Identifies which months drive the most badge engagement

5. How often do users change their avatar?

  1. Avatar change count: profile_avatar_changed total
  2. Avatar change rate: profile_avatar_changed / profile_viewed

6. How many users sign out?

  1. Sign out count: profile_sign_out total (30d)
  2. Sign out rate: profile_sign_out / profile_viewed — high rate may indicate friction
