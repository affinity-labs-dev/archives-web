Questions This Flow Answers

Strategic Questions

  1. How many users open the Today tab each day?
  2. How many users who open the Today tab tap START MY DAY?
  3. How many users complete the full daily story (all 3 sections)?
  4. How many users drop off after WATCH without continuing to EXPLORE?
  5. How many users drop off after EXPLORE without starting QUESTIONS?
  6. How many users return to a completed story and replay it?
  7. How many users tap a past date on the calendar (rewind)?
  8. How many non-subscribers hit the rewind paywall?
  9. How many users purchase a subscription through the daily story rewind paywall?

Behavioral Insights

  1. How many users expand the reading sheet during WATCH?
  2. How many users play the inner voice audio during EXPLORE?
  3. How many users use the back button to revisit a previous section?
  4. How many users dismiss the story from the WATCH section?
  5. How many users dismiss the story from the EXPLORE section?
  6. How many users swipe between cards on the home deck before starting?
  7. How many users view the celebration screen to completion?
  8. How many users close the celebration screen early?
  9. How long do users who complete the story spend on it?
  10. How long do users who abandon the story spend before leaving?
  11. How many users have their video fail to load?

Checkpoint Validation

  1. Today Tab Opened — How many users open the Today tab?
  2. START MY DAY Tapped — How many users actively start the daily story?
  3. WATCH — How many users play the video lesson?
  4. EXPLORE — How many users reach the scrollable reading section?
  5. QUESTIONS — How many users reach the quiz?
  6. Story Completed — How many users finish the full daily story?

Analysis Notes

  • Exclude background dismissals — daily_story_dismissed fires on hook unmount, which includes app backgrounding and tab switching. Filter by completed: false AND time_spent_seconds > 5 to isolate intentional abandons.
  • Replay detection — When progress is 100%, the button reads "RESTART MY DAY" and opens the video modal. There is no separate event for replay vs. first play. Proposed daily_story_started includes is_replay to distinguish.
  • Streak tracking gap — trackStreakIncremented exists in useDailyStoryTracking and AnalyticsService but is never called from today.tsx. Wire it in handleQuizComplete or reportTodayComplete.
  • Rewind paywall deduplication — subscribe_screen_viewed fires once per paywall presentation (re-entrancy guarded by isPaywallPresentedRef). Multiple calendar taps during presentation are blocked.
  • Card viewed debouncing — daily_story_card_viewed fires once per card per session (tracked via cardsSeenRef Set). Story ID change resets the set.
  • Historical content — When a subscriber loads a past date, daily_story_viewed fires with entry_source: "rewind" and is_today: false. The same tracking hook is reused for both flows.
