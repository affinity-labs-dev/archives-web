- **Smart Notification Prompts** - Notification permission prompts at 200 and 750 XP milestones
- **Push Token Sync** - Push notification tokens synced to Customer.io (`cio_push_token` attribute)

- **Subscription Page Compliance** - Added clickable Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links (App Store requirement)

- **Future Day Circles** - Changed to dark navy color (#222446)
- **Achievement Unlocking** - Support both XP formats in achievement system
- **Push Notification Status** - Properly synced to PostHog and Customer.io

### Maintenance
- Updated RiveRuntime to 6.12.3 and rive-react-native to 9.7.1
- Restored DEVELOPMENT_TEAM for NotificationService target
- Various build number bumps (iOS: 143, Android: 44)
